    Evidence at this handoff

      What the skill provides

      Portable instructions, synthetic templates, read-only tracker adaptation and offline evidence checks such as check_records.py. A passing check shows the record is well formed; it cannot prove the evidence is true.

      What this page is not

      Not a scheduler, posting service, follower checker or ManyChat replacement. No account, platform or messaging service is connected, and nothing here was sent. External actions need separate authority and fresh evidence.

      Exceptions stop the flow

      Missing approval, a duplicate response, unknown follower status and a sensitive comment each hold the journey at their gate. Selecting a later stage cannot skip past it.
