# Visual assets and rights

The walkthrough and its preview images (`docs/walkthrough/`) are newly authored. The SVG preview and the HTML are vector markup with no embedded image, font, account identifier or external resource. They use installed or system fonts and the Carbon, Pearl, Champagne, Data Blue and Emerald palette; no logo or trademark is reproduced. The PNG and GIF are rasterised from that SVG and are conceptual, not screen recordings.

UNFI logo/brand rights are reserved to their respective rights holder and are not granted by this package's MIT license. The original branded SVG/PNG and logo are omitted from the reusable candidate because redistribution rights were not established. A separately approved branded presentation may be reviewed later without changing the reusable code license.

No font binaries are shipped. The predecessor's [DM Sans](../assets/fonts/DM-Sans-LICENSE.txt) and [Inter](../assets/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt) SIL Open Font License notices are retained unchanged for provenance; MIT does not replace them.
